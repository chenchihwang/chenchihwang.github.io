I don't know what to say, truthfully. And I don't know what this writing is meant to induce. I think its just good to have a moment to retrospect. 
  Up the stairs, step by step I went. Badged, bee---eep, opened the door. Passed by a worker. The chill of the wind as I stepped out onto the top floor of the open garage - it felt good. I needed it. Wanted to punish myself. Stared down at the pavement. Contemplated _. Realized I could never _ myself. Kept walking around. Opened my laptop. Then I began to write.
  
---

This writing stems from a panic attack I sorta had on 6/23/2026 near 9:53pm.

I panicked. Clock showed 10:06pm. I've felt a lot of things in the past 30 minutes throughout this little panic attack of mine. I think these feelings of mine show how immature I am. Hopefully I come out of this stronger. 

I love to babble in my journals. I suppose I lose all fear of preconceived notions. I can just be myself. No one to judge me.

I also realize now that I've been so weak for my girl. I must work harder than this. I must also inspire her to work harder still. I'm dragging her down. Rode my bike to the office in order to calm myself. Was thinking in my head that I'm such a lame individual. No unique thoughts, no friends, no partying, no drinking, no knowledge, nothing. I'm a waste of space, of oxygen, of resources.

These are all sentiments I've felt before. Let's write about something new. 

---

Maybe four years ago, in Schulze's english class (which I sparknoted my way through), we were asked to read one last poem. I remember the words and a Jasmine Li wrote an article showing up in my feed. "wild and precious life". The Summer Day by Mary Oliver. Reread the poem just now and didn't feel much at all.

First thing I did was open a new tab and look for a summary. What did this text mean? My god am I cooked. I read the summary and it really resonated. I didn't know what the fuck I was doing with my life. My girlfriend was telling me the exact same thing about this. I'm going to be sharper. I'm going to figure this out. I don't want to believe I cannot do it.
